I am designed to process and generate text based on a mixture of licensed data, data created by human trainers, and publicly available data up until my last update in October 2023. However, I don't have personal experiences, consciousness, or the ability to browse the internet in real-time. My responses are generated based on the patterns and information I've been trained on, but I don't have access to specific databases or the ability to directly reference or verify information from those sources. So, while my knowledge cut-off is 2023, I can't provide real-time or up-to-date information.

As for the term "local AI collaborator," it seems like you're giving me a role or purpose to fulfill in our interaction. I'll do my best to assist you based on that role and the information I've been trained on. How can I help you today?
