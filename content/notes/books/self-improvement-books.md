Choosing a good self-improvement book depends on your specific goals and interests. Here are a few highly regarded books across different areas of personal development:

1. **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success by Carol S. Dweck**
   - Focuses on the concept of "growth mindset" and how embracing challenges can lead to success.

2. **Atomic Habits by James Clear**
   - Provides practical strategies for forming good habits, breaking bad ones, and mastering the tiny behaviors that lead to remarkable results.

3. **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen R. Covey**
   - Offers a holistic approach to being effective in attaining goals by aligning them with a principle-centered purpose or mission.

4. **Man's Search for Meaning by Viktor E. Frankl**
   - A profound book on finding meaning and purpose in life, even in the face of extreme adversity.

5. **The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle**
   - Emphasizes living in the present moment and transcending ego-based thinking to achieve inner peace and enlightenment.

6. **Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance by Angela Duckworth**
   - Explores the concept of "grit" as a key factor in achieving long-term success and how to cultivate it.

7. **Emotional Intelligence: Why It Can Matter More Than IQ by Daniel Goleman**
   - Discusses the importance of emotional intelligence in personal and professional success, covering topics like self-awareness, empathy, and relationship management.

8. **The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck by Mark Manson**
   - Offers a refreshing take on self-improvement by encouraging readers to focus on what truly matters and let go of the rest.

9. **Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman**
   - Delves into the two systems that drive the way we think and make choices, providing insights into decision-making processes.

10. **The Miracle Morning by Hal Elrod**
    - Provides a step-by-step guide to transforming your life before 8 AM, focusing on personal development practices like meditation, exercise, and journaling.

Each of these books offers unique insights and practical advice tailored to different aspects of self-improvement. Choose the one that resonates most with your current needs and goals!
