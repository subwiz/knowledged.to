**Anthropology** is the study of humans: who we are, where we came from, how we live, and how our bodies, cultures, languages, and societies have changed over time.

The word comes from Greek roots:

- **anthropos** = human
- **logos** = study or knowledge

So, anthropology literally means **“the study of humans.”**

## What anthropology studies

Anthropology looks at human life in a broad way. It asks questions such as:

- How did humans evolve biologically?
- How do different cultures organize family, religion, politics, and work?
- How do languages shape communication and identity?
- What can ancient tools, bones, buildings, and artifacts tell us about past societies?
- How do people adapt to different environments and historical changes?

## Main branches of anthropology

Anthropology is often divided into four major fields:

### 1. Cultural anthropology

**Cultural anthropology** studies the beliefs, customs, values, rituals, and everyday practices of living human communities.

For example, a cultural anthropologist might study marriage traditions, food practices, religious ceremonies, or how people use technology in a particular society.

### 2. Biological or physical anthropology

**Biological anthropology** studies humans as biological organisms. It focuses on human evolution, genetics, primates, fossils, and physical variation among human populations.

For example, a biological anthropologist might study ancient human skeletons or compare humans with other primates such as chimpanzees.

### 3. Archaeology

**Archaeology** studies past human societies through physical remains, such as tools, pottery, buildings, bones, and other artifacts.

For example, an archaeologist might excavate an ancient settlement to understand how people lived thousands of years ago.

### 4. Linguistic anthropology

**Linguistic anthropology** studies human language and how it relates to culture, thought, identity, and social life.

For example, a linguistic anthropologist might study how different communities use language to show respect, status, gender, or belonging.

## Simple definition

In simple terms, **anthropology is the scientific and humanistic study of human beings across time and place**.

It studies both:

- **past and present humans**
- **biological and cultural aspects of human life**

## Example sentence

> Anthropology helps us understand how humans evolved, how societies differ, and what all human beings have in common.
