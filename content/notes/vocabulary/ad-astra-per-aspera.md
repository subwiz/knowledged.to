_Ad astra per aspera_ is a Latin phrase meaning **“to the stars through hardship”** or **“through difficulties to the stars.”**

- **ad** = “to” or “toward”
- **astra** = “the stars”
- **per** = “through”
- **aspera** = “rough things,” “hardships,” or “difficulties”

The phrase expresses the idea that **great achievements require struggle, perseverance, and endurance**. “The stars” are often used symbolically to mean high goals, success, greatness, or something beyond ordinary reach.

A natural English paraphrase would be:

> We reach great things by going through hard things.

It is commonly used as a motto for schools, military units, organizations, and space-related contexts. A closely related phrase is _per aspera ad astra_, which has the same basic meaning but uses a different word order: **“through hardship to the stars.”**
