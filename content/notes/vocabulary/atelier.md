## Definition

**Atelier** (French) — a workshop or studio, especially one used by an artist, designer, or craftsperson. It also refers to a master artist's studio where assistants and apprentices work and train under the master, as in the traditional **atelier method** of art instruction.

## Etymology

- **Old French** *astelier* — a carpenter's workshop, or a pile of wood shavings
- from **Old French** *astele* — a splinter or chip of wood
- from **Late Latin** *astella* — a variant of Latin *astula* (a splinter or small piece of wood)
- a diminutive of **Latin** *assis* — a board or plank

The root sense ties to wood chips and splinters (the debris of a workshop), which broadened over time to mean the workshop itself, and eventually an artist's studio.
